✦ Day by day ✦

Your Daily
Hair Routine

Simple steps — morning and night — that build healthy hair over time.

☀️
Every
Morning
Daily

Morning Hair Care

1

Scalp Massage — 2 mins

Use your fingertips (not nails) to gently massage your scalp in circular motions. This boosts blood circulation and wakes up hair follicles for better growth.

2

Detangle Gently

Use a wide-tooth comb starting from the ends, working up. Never pull from the roots — this breaks hair and causes unnecessary fall.

3

Light Leave-in or Hair Serum (optional)

If your hair feels dry, apply a tiny amount of argan oil or coconut oil to your ends only — never on the scalp.

4

Loose Style — No Tight Ties

Avoid tight ponytails or buns daily. Tight styles cause breakage and hairline damage. Use a satin scrunchie if tying hair.

🌙
Every
Night
Daily

Nighttime Hair Care

1

Detangle Before Bed

Always detangle before sleep. Sleeping with tangled hair causes major breakage from tossing and turning through the night.

2

Loose Braid or Bun

Put hair in a loose braid before sleeping. This reduces friction and prevents split ends and tangles overnight.

3

Use a Satin/Silk Pillowcase

Cotton absorbs moisture and causes friction. A satin or silk pillowcase keeps hair smooth, reduces frizz, and retains moisture.

4

Hydrate Your Body

Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily. Dehydration directly affects hair dryness and breakage from the inside.

💧
2–3x
Per Week
Weekly

Wash Day Routine

1

Pre-Wash Oil Treatment (30 min before)

Apply warm coconut oil or castor oil to your scalp and hair. Leave 30 mins minimum — this protects hair from shampoo stripping.

2

Shampoo — Scalp Only

Apply shampoo only to your scalp, not the lengths. Let water rinse it through the ends. Use a sulphate-free, gentle shampoo. Over-washing strips natural oils.

3

Conditioner — Lengths & Ends Only

Apply conditioner from mid-lengths to ends. Leave for 3–5 minutes then rinse with cool water. Cool water seals the cuticle for shine.

4

Pat Dry — Never Rub

Use a microfibre towel or old cotton T-shirt. Gently pat and squeeze — never rub. Rubbing causes frizz and breakage when hair is most fragile.

5

Air Dry When Possible

Allow hair to air dry. If using a blow dryer, always use a heat protectant spray first and keep on the lowest heat setting.

Once
Per Week
Weekly

Deep Oil Massage Treatment

1

Warm Your Oil

Warm 2–3 tablespoons of your chosen oil (coconut, castor, olive, or a blend) slightly in a bowl of hot water. Warm oil penetrates deeper into the scalp.

2

Section & Apply

Part hair into 4 sections. Apply oil directly to scalp in each parting, then massage gently with fingertips for 10–15 minutes.

3

Wrap in Warm Towel (Hot Oil Wrap)

Wrap hair in a warm towel for 30–60 minutes or leave overnight. The heat helps the oil absorb deeply and strengthens follicles.

4

Wash Out Thoroughly

Follow with your regular wash day routine to remove the oil completely. If needed, double shampoo your scalp.

DIY Hair Mask Treatments

★

Egg + Olive Oil Mask (Protein Mask)

Mix 1 egg + 2 tbsp olive oil. Apply root to tip. Leave 30 mins. Rinse with cool water (not hot — egg scrambles!). Strengthens and reduces breakage.

★

Banana + Honey Mask (Moisture Mask)

Blend 1 ripe banana + 1 tbsp honey + 1 tbsp coconut oil. Apply to hair. Leave 30–45 mins. Makes hair incredibly soft and shiny.

★

Yoghurt + Lemon Scalp Mask

Mix 4 tbsp plain yoghurt + 1 tsp lemon juice. Apply to scalp only. Leave 20 mins. Excellent for dandruff, itchy scalp, and balancing scalp pH.

Monthly

Monthly Reset Routine

1

Trim Your Ends

Even a tiny ½ cm trim removes split ends and stops them from travelling up the hair shaft. Healthy ends = longer-looking hair.

2

Clarifying Wash

Once a month use a clarifying shampoo to remove buildup from products and hard water minerals from the scalp.

3

Deep Condition for 1 Hour

Apply a rich conditioner or hair mask, cover with a shower cap, and leave for 60 minutes. This restores moisture lost from the whole month.

✦ Nature's best ✦

Best Oils for
Your Hair

These are the most effective, proven natural oils — what they do and exactly how to use them.

🥥
Coconut Oil
All-rounder

The only oil that penetrates the hair shaft. Reduces protein loss, prevents breakage, adds deep shine and controls frizz.

Warm slightly. Apply to scalp & hair. Leave 30 min–overnight. Use 1–2x week.

🫒
Castor Oil
Growth Booster

Ricinoleic acid improves blood flow to scalp. Thickens hair, fills in sparse areas, and dramatically reduces hair fall over 6–8 weeks.

Mix with coconut oil (50:50). Massage scalp. Leave 2–4 hours. Use 1x week.

🌾
Argan Oil
Shine & Softness

"Liquid gold." Rich in Vitamin E and fatty acids. Makes hair incredibly silky, tames frizz, and protects from heat and humidity.

2–3 drops on palms. Apply to damp hair ends only. Use daily as leave-in.

🌿
Rosemary Oil
Stimulates Growth

Studies show it works as well as minoxidil for hair regrowth. Activates dormant follicles and significantly reduces shedding.

5–6 drops in 2 tbsp coconut oil. Massage scalp. Leave 1–2 hours. Use 2x week.

🌸
Olive Oil
Deep Conditioning

Excellent emollient. Softens coarse, dry hair and adds moisture. Contains Vitamin E and squalene — great for dry scalp too.

Warm and apply generously. Wrap in towel 1 hour. Use as monthly deep treatment.

🌻
Onion Seed Oil (Kalonji)
Fall Reduction

Used in traditional medicine for centuries. Thymoquinone in kalonji significantly reduces hair fall and encourages regrowth.

Massage into scalp. Leave 1–2 hours or overnight. Wash out. Use 2x week.

✦ Important ✦

Do's & Don'ts for
Healthy Hair

Small habits make the biggest difference. These are the most important things to follow — and avoid.

✅ Always Do These

🌿Sleep on a satin or silk pillowcase every night
🌿Drink 8+ glasses of water daily for scalp hydration
🌿Detangle from ends to roots — always start at the bottom
🌿Use cool or lukewarm water for the final rinse
🌿Pat dry with a microfibre towel after washing
🌿Massage scalp for 2 mins daily to boost circulation
🌿Trim ends every 6–8 weeks to prevent split ends spreading
🌿Cover hair with a light scarf in harsh sun or pollution
🌿Eat a protein-rich meal at least once a day
🌿Allow hair to air dry as often as possible

❌ Never Do These

🚫Never rub hair with a towel — always pat gently
🚫Never wash hair with very hot water — it strips natural oils
🚫Never brush wet hair — use wide-tooth comb only when damp
🚫Never sleep with hair tied tightly — causes breakage
🚫Avoid sulphate shampoos — they are too harsh and strip hair
🚫Avoid heat styling (straighteners, curlers) without heat protectant
🚫Don't over-wash — 2–3 times per week maximum
🚫Avoid tight hairstyles daily — they damage the hairline
🚫Never apply conditioner to the scalp — only lengths and ends
🚫Avoid crash diets — sudden nutrition drops cause major hair fall

🧠 Lifestyle Tips That Directly Affect Hair

😴

Sleep 7–8 hours — hair grows while you sleep. Chronic sleep deprivation leads to hormonal imbalance and hair fall.

🧘

Manage stress — stress is one of the biggest causes of sudden hair shedding (telogen effluvium). Prayer, walks, breathing exercises help.

🚶

Exercise regularly — even a 20-minute walk daily improves blood circulation to the scalp and supports hair growth hormones.

☀️

Sunlight daily — 15–20 minutes of sun exposure boosts Vitamin D, which is directly tied to hair follicle health.

🚿

Keep your scalp clean — a clean, healthy scalp is the foundation. Buildup from products and hard water blocks follicles.
